The Problem

Import Dependency

Bread consumption is high, yet Indonesia relies heavily on imported wheat flour to meet demand. A supply chain vulnerable to global price swings.

Health concerns

Standard wheat bread is low in fiber and carries a high glycemic index, and it's simply off-limits for anyone with gluten sensitivity.

Underused local commodities

Abundant local staples like banana and tempeh remain underused as ingredients for modern bakery products.

Our Solution

A loaf built on local, gluten-free ingredients.

We swap wheat flour for local alternative flours, held together without gluten.

Banana flour
Base substitute for wheat, locally abundant.
Xanthan gum
Binds the matrix in place of gluten.

Regular bread depends on gluten to trap gas and hold its shape. Without wheat, that job falls to xanthan gum — a binding agent that lets banana and tempeh flour rise and set like a proper loaf.

The result is a bread that's local by ingredient, gentler on the glycemic index, and safe for people avoiding gluten — without asking anyone to give up the everyday loaf.

STEM Approaches

How you can try it at home:

1

Dissolve the granulated sugar and instant yeast in warm milk. Let stand for 5–10 minutes until foamy.

2

Add the egg yolk to the yeast mixture and stir until well combined.

3

In a separate bowl, combine the all-purpose flour, banana flour, xanthan gum, and salt. Mix well.

4

Gradually pour the liquid yeast mixture into the dry flour mixture.

5

Knead by hand or with a mixer until combined, then add the margarine.

6

Knead until the dough is soft and well combined.

7

Place the dough in a proofing box equipped with an IoT sensor (Arduino/ESP32).

8

Close the lid tightly and let it proof for 45–60 minutes at the optimal temperature. Record temperature and humidity data in real time.

9

Gently deflate the dough to release CO2 gas.

10

Divide the dough into equal portions, add chocolate spread (as shown in the video) to the center of each, then shape into balls.

11

Arrange on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper or greased with margarine. Let rest again for 15–20 minutes (second proofing).

12

Bake in a preheated oven at 175°C–180°C for 18–22 minutes until golden brown (Maillard reaction).

13

Immediately brush the surface of the bread with butter or margarine after removing it from the oven to make the surface shiny and soft.
